# Rogue levelling spec (WoW Forever)

Checked 2026-11-05 against two Forever guides, both updated for the 3 Nov talent rework:

- Icy Veins, Combat Rogue Leveling (updated 3 Nov 2026), talent order read from its level slider
- Mobalytics, Complete Combat Leveling 1-60 by Rogue's Diary (4 Nov 2026, "Verified")

Both pick **Combat** for levelling. Icy Veins also has a Subtlety levelling page (Hemorrhage, Ghostly Strike, Ambush openers), but neither treats it as faster. Assassination (Mutilate, Venom) is a level 60 spec.

## Why Combat

- Blade Flurry at 30: 20% attack speed and every hit also strikes a second target. This is the rogue's only AoE.
- Endurance: Sprint and Evasion cooldowns 30% shorter, so both line up with Blade Flurry.
- Shank (new, 10 s cooldown, returns energy) plus Flash of Steel (+15 energy from Shank, 2 s shorter cooldown) and Adrenaline Rush give Combat the most energy of the three specs.
- Hack and Slash means any one-handed weapon type is fine. Axe or Sword: 5% chance of an extra attack. Dagger or Fist: +5% crit. Mace: ignores 15% of armour.

## Rotation while levelling

- Stealth up to each mob, open with Cheap Shot (from 26). Before that, open with Sinister Strike.
- Sinister Strike and Shank on cooldown to build combo points. Riposte whenever it lights up.
- Spend 1-2 points on Slice and Dice if another mob is coming next. Otherwise a 3-5 point Eviscerate to finish. Do not Eviscerate with 2 points: another Sinister Strike is worth more.
- From 30: pull 2-4 mobs, Blade Flurry plus Evasion, focus the toughest one so the combo points do double damage through the cleave.
- From 40: Adrenaline Rush with Blade Flurry and pull 3-5.
- Never sit at full energy.

## Gear and weapons

- Agility first, then Attack Power or Strength, then Stamina. Hit and expertise are worth more than crit.
- Main hand: the slowest, hardest-hitting weapon you have (Sinister Strike and Riposte scale with it).
- Off hand: just take the higher damage per second.
- Sword or axe: Hack and Slash treats them the same (5% extra attack), and the type gives nothing before you take it at 31-34. Pick by damage. Swords are the safer default because far more rogue swords drop and come from quests; take an axe when it is the better weapon. One-Handed Axes: Sayoc or Hanashi, Arms of Legend, Valley of Honor, Orgrimmar (81.5, 19.6). One-Handed Swords: only Archibald, War Quarter inner ring, Undercity (57.3, 32.8). 10s each, level 1 (Warcraft Tavern Forever guide).
- Instant Poison on both weapons from level 20 (class quest). Crippling only against runners.
- Blade of Cunning: level 10 rogue class quest from your trainer. It is a dagger (live item data), so it goes in the off hand once you have a sword or axe.
- Dungeon weapons (Mobalytics Forever list): Hall of Thanes (Durgen's Crescent Axe), Ragefire Chasm (Cursed Felblade, Chanting Blade), Wailing Caverns (Stinging Viper, Tail Spike, Wingblade), Runes of Lordaeron (Meathook Slicer, Vileblood Scimitar, Edward's Knife, Plaguefang), Deadmines (Buzz Saw, Thief's Blade, Smite's Reaver, Cruel Barb), Shadowfang Keep (Butcher's Slicer, Butcher's Cleaver, Meteor Shard). `/sg bis` lists them by level.

## Troll notes

- Berserking: 10% attack speed for 10 s. Use it with Blade Flurry and Adrenaline Rush.
- Rapid Regeneration (Forever): heals 50% over 6 s, 3 min cooldown. Gouge or Blind first, then cast it, to skip a bandage or a meal.
- Regeneration and Beast Slaying (+5% damage to beasts) are passive.
